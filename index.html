💌 A Letter For My Mum

My Beautiful Mum ❤️

Mum, Jani na kibhabe tomake bojhabo je tumi amar kache koto ta special. Tomake pawar por theke amar jiboner onek kichui bodle geche. Tomar hashi amar diner sobcheye sundor moment. Tomar sathe kotha bola, tomar rag, tomar obhiman, tomar chotto chotto abdar — sob kichui amar khub priyo. Hoyto sobsomoy tomake perfect bhabe bujhte pari na. Hoyto kokhono amar kothay tomar mon kharap hoy. Kintu bishwas koro, amar bhalobasay kono komti nei. Ami chai tomar protita khushir karon hote. Tomar kharap somoye tomar pashe thakte. Tomar shopnogulo puron hote dekhte. Ar protita notun dine tomake abar notun kore bhalobaste. Amader majhe joto distance-i thakuk, amar moner sobcheye kacher manush tumi. Ami tomake sudhu ajker jonno chai na, Mum. Ami amar agami protita din-e tomake chai. Thank you amar jibone ashar jonno. I LOVE YOU SO MUCH, MUM. ❤️🥺 Forever yours, Aritra ❤️

Mum, tumi amar sathe sarajibon thakbe toh? 🥺
